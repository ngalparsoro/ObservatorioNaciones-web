Sources

All the Observatory's data come from official public sources: the Ministry of the Interior, the National Statistics Institute (INE) and, for regional elections, the government or statistics institute of each community. Nothing is made up or estimated: every result is the one published for each polling station or municipality, added up by section, municipality and area.
