How to use

A tour of just over a minute, in nine steps, to read the elections nation by nation.

At the bottom (at the top on mobile), the timeline, to scale and up to today: drag the triangle, ▶ moves forward month by month (key P) and keys Q and E jump to the previous and the next election. To its right, the search (key F) finds an election by month, year or, for regional elections, community.

In the tools on the left, Elections switches from Congress to the regional elections (key A): on the timeline, each community with the parliament in force at that moment; in the search, a day or a single community, and the map shows only that. The video shows Congress.

On the map, the ? key opens the help with all the keyboard shortcuts.
